About Us





Welcome to CineIQ
A playful playground for cinephiles!
This site was created as part of our group assignment to blend creativity, coding, and a love for cinema into one interactive experience. Here, you can test your knowledge across multiple genres, challenge your friends, and discover new films, actors, and iconic moments. With a clean and modern interface, fun quizzes, and a competitive leaderboard, Movie Trivia Hub celebrates the magic of cinema while giving you an engaging and playful way to explore it. Whether you’re a casual viewer or a die-hard film fan, this is the place to prove your movie mastery and immerse yourself in the world of films.
